The problem

Kannada school books are entirely
in Kannada. Some parents can't read them.

Thousands of families in Karnataka — and in the Kannada diaspora worldwide — face this every day. Kannada is compulsory in school, but tools to help at home simply don't exist.

📚

Homework comes home. And no one can help.

Activity books, homework sheets, and school poems are in Kannada script. For parents who grew up speaking a different language, helping with homework isn't straightforward.

Families in Bengaluru who speak Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, or English face this every single day.
🔍

Google Translate isn't a solution

Parents use Google Translate, Google Lens, and Search — switching between apps, word by word, page by page. No single platform gives both pronunciation in English and meaning in English, together.

You shouldn't need 4 apps just to help your child read one page of homework.
😟

Poems & passages are the hardest

A Kannada school poem is a real challenge — unfamiliar letters, no pronunciation guide, no translation. Children can't memorise what they don't understand.

Akshara lets you scan a poem and read it with pronunciation and meaning, so your child can memorise it.
